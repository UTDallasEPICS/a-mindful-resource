About A Mindful Resource

Making mental wellness easier to reach.

A Mindful Resource helps connect communities with mental health support through trust, accessibility, and culturally aligned care.

Our Purpose

Care before crisis.

A Mindful Resource focuses on helping people connect with mental wellness support before they reach a crisis point.

The organization works to reduce barriers to care by building trust, connecting people with resources, and supporting communities that may otherwise struggle to access culturally relevant mental health services.

What Guides the Work

Support rooted in community.

Trust

Creating relationships and environments where people feel comfortable seeking help.

Access

Making mental health resources easier to discover, understand, and reach.

Community

Connecting care with the communities and spaces where people already live and build relationships.

DJ

Founder

Dominique Johnson

MSN, RN · Founder & CEO

Dominique Johnson founded A Mindful Resource with a vision of making mental wellness more accessible and connected to the communities it serves.

His work emphasizes a trust-first approach to mental health: meeting people where they are, reducing barriers to support, and building stronger pathways between communities and care.
