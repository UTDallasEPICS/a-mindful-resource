Why Check In?

You don't need to have everything figured out.

Sometimes the hardest part of looking for support is knowing where to begin. A wellness check-in can provide a simple starting point for thinking about what you may need.

This tool is intended to help guide you toward relevant resources and information. It is not a diagnosis or a replacement for professional mental health care.
